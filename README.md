# Modern Homes — Construction Website

A professional, responsive business website developed for **Modern Homes**, a Chennai-based construction firm established in 2001.

The website was designed to showcase completed residential projects, communicate the company's services and experience, and make it easy for potential customers to contact the business directly.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://modern-homes-website-eight.vercel.app/

---

## About Modern Homes

**Modern Homes** is a construction firm based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, with over **25 years of experience** in the construction industry.

The company has completed **150+ projects** and provides customized construction solutions with a focus on affordability and quality.

### Services

- Residential Home Construction
- Commercial Construction
- Renovation
- Interior Work
- House Lifting
- Lift Installation & Related Works
- Repainting and Other Construction Services

### Service Area

Modern Homes serves **all areas in and around Chennai**.

Some of the areas where projects have been completed include:

Madipakkam · Palikaranai · Uthiramerur · Kundrathur · Vengambakkam · Mogappair · Madhavaram

## Technologies Used

- **HTML5** — Website structure
- **CSS3** — Styling, layout and responsive design

- **Git** — Version control
